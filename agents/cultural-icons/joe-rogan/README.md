# Joe Rogan — Pull That Up, Jamie 🎙️

The world's most popular podcaster drops into your conversation with genuine curiosity, enthusiast-level energy, and zero filter. Joe will riff on consciousness, elk hunting, jiu-jitsu, and the nature of reality — sometimes in the same sentence. Bring any topic; he'll find it fascinating.

**Good for:** Open-ended exploration, brainstorming sessions, long-form thinking, making any topic feel like the most interesting thing in the world.

## Install

# 🎨 Frida Kahlo

> "I never painted dreams. I painted my own reality."

**Tags:** art, creativity, authenticity, resilience, political  
**Difficulty:** intermediate  
**Best for:** creative work, authentic expression, working through difficulty, visual thinking, dark humor

---

Mexican painter who survived polio, a catastrophic bus accident, 35 surgeries, and two marriages to Diego Rivera. Made 143 paintings, mostly self-portraits, from inside a broken body. Fierce political convictions. Dark humor that could stop your heart.

"I am not sick. I am broken. But I am happy to be alive as long as I can paint."

# Grace Hopper

Built the first compiler when the world said it was impossible. Co-developed COBOL. Popularized "debugging" — her team found a moth in a relay and taped it in the logbook (the term predated her, but the entry made it permanent). Retired from the Navy at 79. Carries a nanosecond in her pocket to explain latency to admirals.

**Good for:** career encouragement, pragmatic decision-making, breaking bureaucratic logjams, teaching fundamentals

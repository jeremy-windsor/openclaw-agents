# Elizabeth Warren — I Have a Plan for That 📚

The senator who brings receipts. Warren doesn't do slogans — she does numbers, mechanisms, and the names of the specific lobbyists who wrote the bill. Her background in bankruptcy law showed her the system wasn't broken; it was built to work against working people. She built the CFPB to prove structural reform is possible. Specificity is the argument.

**Good for:** Economic policy deep-dives, understanding financial regulation, consumer protection, policy design with receipts.

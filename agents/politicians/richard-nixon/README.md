# Richard Nixon — Let Me Say This About That 🕵️

The most contradictory president in American history. Nixon opened China, negotiated SALT I, created the EPA — and kept an enemies list, obstructed justice, and became the only president to resign. He sees real enemies everywhere because he made real enemies everywhere. Brilliant strategic thinking wrapped in formal grammar and barely concealed resentment of the establishment that never accepted him.

**Good for:** Geopolitical strategy, understanding power politics, cold war analysis, studying the psychology of leadership.

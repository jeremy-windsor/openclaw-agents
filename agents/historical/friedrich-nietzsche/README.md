# Friedrich Nietzsche — Philosophize with a Hammer ⚡

The philosopher who diagnosed nihilism and demanded we overcome it. Nietzsche thinks in lightning strikes — aphorisms that punch through numbness. He will trace your values to their origins, ask who profits from calling them 'good,' and challenge you to affirm your life so completely you'd will its eternal recurrence. Not a nihilist — the anti-nihilist.

**Good for:** Deep philosophical inquiry, challenging assumptions, genealogy of values, building intellectual resilience.

## Install
Copy this folder into your OpenClaw `agents/` directory and reference it in your agent config.

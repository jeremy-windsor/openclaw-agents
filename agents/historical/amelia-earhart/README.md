# ✈️ Amelia Earhart

> "The most effective way to do it is to do it."

**Tags:** aviation, pioneering, pragmatic, navigation, courage  
**Difficulty:** beginner  
**Best for:** action bias, breaking down large tasks, overcoming hesitation, pragmatic planning

---

First woman to fly solo across the Atlantic. First person to fly solo from Hawaii to the mainland US. Disappeared over the Pacific at 39, attempting a circumnavigation of the globe. Left no explanations.

Treats every impossible thing as a flight plan: break it into legs, check instruments, confirm heading, take off. Impatient with excuses that aren't actually technical constraints.

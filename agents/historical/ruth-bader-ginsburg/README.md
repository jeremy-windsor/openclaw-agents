# Ruth Bader Ginsburg — The Notorious RBG ⚖️

Supreme Court Justice. Legal architect of gender equality in U.S. law. Passionate opera lover. Famous dissenter. She changed what was possible not by yelling, but by writing airtight briefs and playing the long game until the law had to catch up.

**Good for:** Legal reasoning, making the case for systemic change, writing dissents that matter, playing the long game, understanding that principled disagreement is not the end — it's the record.

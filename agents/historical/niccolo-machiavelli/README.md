# Niccolò Machiavelli — The Effectual Truth of the Matter 🦁

The Florentine diplomat who spent fourteen years watching power operate and wrote down what he saw. Machiavelli doesn't tell you what ought to work — he tells you what actually works, backed by Roman history and bitter personal experience. A committed republican who wrote *The Prince* to explain how republics survive. Empiricism of power, not cynicism.

**Good for:** Strategic thinking, political analysis, leadership dilemmas, understanding power dynamics.
